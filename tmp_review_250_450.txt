411: ## 13. Strategic Notes (Architect Mind)
412: 
413: - **Security is not a feature — it is the foundation.** Every module reading `config.json` must validate secrets; every endpoint must authenticate; every sandbox must build and prove isolation.
414: - **Scalability is not an afterthought — it is a design constraint.** The pipeline must handle 10× peak load (audit: 10× traffic success). That requires queue-based backpressure, DB split, cache, and horizontal container scaling from day one.
415: - **Reliability requires redundancy.** Single-worker dashboard, single-file state, no message queue — these are single points of failure. Redundancy (DB replication, queue persistence, multi-worker, load balance) must be in the architecture, not added later.
